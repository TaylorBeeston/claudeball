## Other sources (see later sections of `README.md`)
Procedural assets (field, stadium, bat, ball, uniforms, gear) are generated by the scripts in `src/` and have no third-party content. The photographic texture sets that are used are listed below.

## ambientCG PBR texture sets (uniform fabrics, leather, rubber, plastic, turf, clay, stone, concrete)
All sets are **CC0 1.0** (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/, downloaded without an account from https://ambientcg.com/view?id=<ID>).
