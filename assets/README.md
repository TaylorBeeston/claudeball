# Claudeball assets

> **Change (axis flip):** all field/stadium geometry and `field_layout.json` were mirrored `x → -x` relative to the first release (which had 1B at +X). Node names keep their real-world meaning (`Base_1B`, `Bullpen_R`, `FoulPole_R`, right field…), so `Base_1B` is now at `-X`. Players are unchanged (they face +Z with left = +X). A right-handed batter therefore stands at the `+X` side of the plate facing `-X` (yaw −90° about Y), with the pitcher (+Z) on his left, which matches the `swing` clip.

Generated with Blender 5.2 (via the Blender MCP) from the scripts in `src/` — the scripts are the source of truth
(`.blend` files are not committed; procedural textures are generated with numpy and embedded in the GLBs).
Re-run: in Blender, `CB_SRC="<repo>/assets/src"; exec(open(CB_SRC+"/ball.py").read())` (same for bat/field/stadium/players).

## Conventions (all GLBs)
- Units: meters. glTF Y-up. **Origin = home plate apex (back tip of the plate)**, `+Y` up, `+Z` toward center field, **`+X` toward THIRD base (first base at `-X`)** — right-handed, so from behind home plate first base is on the right.
- `ball.glb` / `bat.glb` are object-local (origin at ball center / bat knob) so the engine positions them from the sim.
- Textures are embedded JPEG (albedo + normal). Materials are glTF PBR; ground materials also use `COLOR_0` vertex colors (mowing stripes) multiplied with tiled world-space UVs (`uv = xz / 4 m`), so the texture repeats every 4 m — use `RepeatWrapping`.
- Ground layers are stacked a few mm apart (0, 0.010, 0.020, 0.026, 0.034 m); use `logarithmicDepthBuffer` or `polygonOffset` if you see z-fighting at distance.

## ball.glb
Node `ball` (leather, r = 36.9 mm → 73.8 mm diameter) with child `ball_stitches` (108 red V-stitches as real geometry). ~6k tris. Spin about any axis; origin at center.

## bat.glb
Node `bat`, 0.864 m (34 in) long. **Origin at the knob; barrel extends along local +Y** (glTF). Max barrel diameter 66.6 mm, handle 23 mm. Drive it by placing the origin at the sim's hands/knob position and orienting +Y toward the bat tip.

## field.glb  (see also `field_layout.json` for exact numbers: bases, fence polyline, foul poles, ground outline)
Nodes: `Grass_Outfield`, `Grass_Infield`, `Dirt`, `Dirt_Cutouts` (home circle, base cutouts), `WarningTrack` (15 ft), `Mound` (18 ft dia, 10 in high), `PitchersRubber` (front edge 60 ft 6 in), `HomePlate`, `Base_1B/2B/3B` (15 in), `Chalk` (foul lines, batter's/catcher's boxes, 3 ft lane, coach boxes, on-deck circles), `FoulPole_L/R`, `Dugout_1B/3B` (shells), `Bullpen_L/R` (+`_Plate`).
Fence: distance from apex = `330 ft + 70 ft * cos(2a)` where `a` is the angle from the center-field line (330 ft at poles, 400 ft to center), listed in `field_layout.json`.
Approximations: coach-box and catcher's-box placement, dugout/bullpen placement and foul-territory outline are plausible, not surveyed.

## stadium.glb  (same origin/axes as field.glb — load both at the origin; seat instance positions were mirrored accordingly)
Generated by `src/stadium_gen.py` (numpy) + `src/stadium.py`. ~105k static tris + ~23k instanced seats (10 tris each).
- `Bowl` — one lofted mesh for the 3-tier bowl (treads/risers/back walls; 2 materials: `concrete`, `fascia_dark`). Infield stands have 3 tiers; they taper to a single outfield bleacher tier at the foul poles. Has `COLOR_0` (dark in the batter's-eye zone).
- `Wall_Padding` (dark green, 1.1 m in foul territory → 2.44 m in the outfield), `Wall_YellowLine`, `BattersEye` (12 m dark wall behind CF).
- `Seats_T1`, `Seats_T2`, `Seats_T3` — **seat templates using `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing`** (three.js GLTFLoader turns them into `InstancedMesh`; no seat is a separate node). Seat template faces +Z (center field), instances are rotated about Y to face the field.
- `BackstopNetting` (alpha-blended net texture) + `NetPosts`, `PressBox` (glass band behind home), `Scoreboard` + `Scoreboard_Screen` (emissive; swap its map for a live CanvasTexture — UVs span 0..1), `DugoutRoof_1B/3B`, `LightTower_1..6` + `LightTower_n_Lamps` (emissive; add real lights if wanted).
- Wall/bowl follow `field_layout.json` → `ground_outline` (the field's grass polygon ends at the wall).
- Not done: baked AO, LOD variants, KTX2/meshopt (no toolchain installed; textures are JPEG, geometry is uncompressed), crowd, ad boards beyond the fascia bands.

## players/  (`player_base.glb`, `player_home`, `player_away`, `player_batter`, `player_catcher`, `player_umpire`)
Stylized-realistic mannequin (~4k tris body, 1.85 m), one skin, generated by `src/player_build.py` (+ `player_rig.py`, `player_anim.py`, `player_clips.py`).
- Skeleton: 22 Mixamo-named bones `mixamorig:Hips, Spine, Spine1, Spine2, Neck, Head, {Left,Right}{Shoulder,Arm,ForeArm,Hand,UpLeg,Leg,Foot,ToeBase}` (no fingers/eyes). three.js strips `:` from node names → `mixamorigHips`, etc.
- Model faces **+Z** (glTF), character-left = **+X**, feet at y = 0. Right-handed pitcher/thrower/batter; glove on the left hand (mirror with scale.x = -1 for lefties). Attach the ball to `RightHand`, the bat to `RightHand` (both hands hold it in `swing`; bat +Y along the barrel).
- Clips (30 fps, exact names): `idle`(loop) `windup` `pitch`(release ≈ frame 20) `swing`(contact ≈ frame 21, batter's pitcher-side is model-left) `run`(loop) `field_ready`(loop) `field_catch` `throw`(release ≈ frame 12) `slide`(root motion baked) `celebrate`(loop) `catcher_crouch`(loop). `pitch`/`throw`/`slide` include hip translation (root motion).
- Materials to recolor/swap: `skin`, `uniform_jersey`, `uniform_pants`, `uniform_socks`, `cleats`, `cap`, `helmet`, `glove`, `catcher_gear`. Gear nodes (skinned): `Gear_Cap`, `Gear_Helmet`, `Gear_Glove`, `Gear_CatcherMask`, `Gear_ChestProtector`, `Gear_ShinGuard_L/R`. `player_base.glb` contains all gear (hide what you don't need); the other files are pre-configured (home/away colors, batter=helmet, catcher=full gear, umpire=dark outfit).
- Face/hair/number: `Face_Details` (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, ears; skinned to Head), `Face_Hair` (hide under caps if desired), and jersey numbers as two textured quads on the back, `Gear_Number_Tens` / `Gear_Number_Ones` (material `jersey_number`, alpha-blended white digits from `players/number_digits.png`, 10 digits side by side; UV spans one 0.1-wide cell, currently "2" and "7"). To show digit `d` set `texture.offset.x = (d - default)/10` (tens default 2, ones default 7) with `RepeatWrapping` off / clamp, and tint via material color. Umpire has no number.
- Limitations: no fingers, stylized (not detailed) faces, flat-color uniforms with fabric/skin normal maps; animations are procedural FK/IK key poses (Bezier-interpolated), serviceable but not mocap quality.

## Textures: RepeatWrapping (important)
`grass`, `dirt`, `track` (field) and `concrete` (stadium) are 2K **tileable** textures sampled with world-space UVs (`uv = xz / 4 m`, so one repeat = 4 m). Set `texture.wrapS = texture.wrapT = THREE.RepeatWrapping` on `map` and `normalMap` after loading (GLTFLoader keeps the sampler's wrap mode, which Blender exports as repeat, but set it explicitly if you re-use the textures).

## Optimized + LOD builds (`optimized/`)
`./optimize.sh` (gltf-transform: meshopt geometry compression + WebP textures; node names and instancing are preserved, meshes are NOT joined):
- `optimized/{ball,bat,field,stadium}.glb`, `optimized/players/*.glb` — use with `GLTFLoader.setMeshoptDecoder(MeshoptDecoder)` (three/examples/jsm/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js). WebP textures need no extra setup in modern browsers.
- `optimized/lod1/stadium.glb` — far LOD without instanced seats/net posts, simplified 50%. `optimized/lod1/*.glb` (players) — 512 px textures, simplified to ~30% tris.
- KTX2/Basis was **not** produced: the `toktx` tool is not installed here. If you install KTX-Software, add `--texture-compress ktx2` to `optimize.sh`.
- The un-compressed GLBs next to this README remain the reference (no decoder needed).

## Extras
- `ads/ad_atlas.png` — 3×2 ad-panel atlas (1536×256, six fictional sponsors). Used by the `AdBoards` node in `stadium.glb` (3 m panels cycling along the outfield wall, 0.35–1.25 m high, in front of `Wall_Padding`). Swap the map to re-skin.
- `crowd/crowd_sheet.png` — 2048×1024 RGBA sheet, 8×4 cells of 256×256, transparent seated-spectator cutouts (bust, varied skin/shirt/cap/raised arm). Intended use: instanced billboard quads (pick a random cell per spectator; rows are visually interchangeable) on the `Seats_T*` instance positions (`src/seats.npz`, pre-mirror x) — not placed in the GLB to keep it light. Regenerate with `python3 src/gen_2d.py assets/`.
- Sky HDRI (not committed, CC0): Poly Haven `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` — https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky (use the 2k `.hdr`, load with `RGBELoader`, set as `scene.environment` and `background`). For a night game try `https://polyhaven.com/hdris/night` sets.
