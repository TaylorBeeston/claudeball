# Claudeball assets

> **Change (axis flip):** all field/stadium geometry and `field_layout.json` were mirrored `x → -x` relative to the first release (which had 1B at +X). Node names keep their real-world meaning (`Base_1B`, `Bullpen_R`, `FoulPole_R`, right field…), so `Base_1B` is now at `-X`. Players are unchanged (they face +Z with left = +X). A right-handed batter therefore stands at the `+X` side of the plate facing `-X` (yaw −90° about Y), with the pitcher (+Z) on his left, which matches the `swing` clip.

Generated with Blender 5.2 (via the Blender MCP) from the scripts in `src/` — the scripts are the source of truth
(`.blend` files are not committed; procedural textures are generated with numpy and embedded in the GLBs).
Re-run: in Blender, `CB_SRC="<repo>/assets/src"; exec(open(CB_SRC+"/ball.py").read())` (same for bat/field/stadium/players).

## Conventions (all GLBs)
- Units: meters. glTF Y-up. **Origin = home plate apex (back tip of the plate)**, `+Y` up, `+Z` toward center field, **`+X` toward THIRD base (first base at `-X`)** — right-handed, so from behind home plate first base is on the right.
- `ball.glb` / `bat.glb` are object-local (origin at ball center / bat knob) so the engine positions them from the sim.
- Textures are embedded JPEG (albedo + normal). Materials are glTF PBR; ground materials also use `COLOR_0` vertex colors (mowing stripes) multiplied with tiled world-space UVs (`uv = xz / 4 m`), so the texture repeats every 4 m — use `RepeatWrapping`.
- Ground layers are stacked a few mm apart (0, 0.010, 0.020, 0.026, 0.034 m); use `logarithmicDepthBuffer` or `polygonOffset` if you see z-fighting at distance.

## ball.glb
Node `ball` (leather, r = 36.9 mm → 73.8 mm diameter) with child `ball_stitches` (108 red V-stitches as real geometry). ~6k tris. Spin about any axis; origin at center.

## bat.glb
Node `bat`, 0.864 m (34 in) long. **Origin at the knob; barrel extends along local +Y** (glTF). Max barrel diameter 66.6 mm, handle 23 mm. Drive it by placing the origin at the sim's hands/knob position and orienting +Y toward the bat tip.

## players/  (`player_base.glb`, `player_home`, `player_away`, `player_batter`, `player_catcher`, `player_umpire`)
Stylized-realistic mannequin (~4k tris body, 1.85 m), one skin, generated by `src/player_build.py` (+ `player_rig.py`, `player_anim.py`, `player_clips.py`).
- Skeleton: 22 Mixamo-named bones `mixamorig:Hips, Spine, Spine1, Spine2, Neck, Head, {Left,Right}{Shoulder,Arm,ForeArm,Hand,UpLeg,Leg,Foot,ToeBase}` (no fingers/eyes). three.js strips `:` from node names → `mixamorigHips`, etc.
- Model faces **+Z** (glTF), character-left = **+X**, feet at y = 0. Right-handed pitcher/thrower/batter; glove on the left hand (mirror with scale.x = -1 for lefties). Attach the ball to `RightHand`, the bat to `RightHand` (both hands hold it in `swing`; bat +Y along the barrel).
- Clips (**24 fps**, exact names; see "Clip list" below for durations): `idle`(loop) `windup` `pitch`(release ≈ frame 20) `swing`(contact ≈ frame 21, batter's pitcher-side is model-left) `run`(loop) `field_ready`(loop) `field_catch` `throw`(release ≈ frame 12) `slide`(root motion baked) `celebrate`(loop) `catcher_crouch`(loop), plus the third-pass clips `batting_stance` `trot` `run_turn` `catch_jump`. `pitch`/`throw`/`slide`/`catch_jump` include hip translation (root motion).
- Materials to recolor/swap: `skin`, `uniform_jersey`, `uniform_pants`, `uniform_socks`, `cleats`, `cap`, `helmet`, `glove`, `catcher_gear`. Gear nodes (skinned): `Gear_Cap`, `Gear_Helmet`, `Gear_Glove`, `Gear_CatcherMask`, `Gear_ChestProtector`, `Gear_ShinGuard_L/R`. `player_base.glb` contains all gear (hide what you don't need); the other files are pre-configured (home/away colors, batter=helmet, catcher=full gear, umpire=dark outfit).
- Face/hair/number: `Face_Details` (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, ears; skinned to Head), `Face_Hair` (hide under caps if desired), and jersey numbers as two textured quads on the back, `Gear_Number_Tens` / `Gear_Number_Ones` (material `jersey_number`, alpha-blended white digits from `players/number_digits.png`, 10 digits side by side; UV spans one 0.1-wide cell, currently "2" and "7"). To show digit `d` set `texture.offset.x = (d - default)/10` (tens default 2, ones default 7) with `RepeatWrapping` off / clamp, and tint via material color. Umpire has no number.
- Limitations: no fingers, stylized (not detailed) faces, flat-color uniforms with fabric/skin normal maps; animations are procedural FK/IK key poses (Bezier-interpolated), serviceable but not mocap quality.

## Textures: RepeatWrapping (important)
`grass`, `dirt`, `track` (field) and `concrete` (stadium) are 2K **tileable** textures sampled with world-space UVs (`uv = xz / 4 m`, so one repeat = 4 m). Set `texture.wrapS = texture.wrapT = THREE.RepeatWrapping` on `map` and `normalMap` after loading (GLTFLoader keeps the sampler's wrap mode, which Blender exports as repeat, but set it explicitly if you re-use the textures).

### Head / neck rig (checked, no rig problem found)
- Rest pose: `Neck` (joint at y = 1.49 m) and `Head` (joint at y = 1.585 m) both have **identity world rotation at rest**: local +X = character-left (+X world), +Y = up, +Z = forward (+Z world, the way the face looks); bone length along +Y. All release frames are within 0.35-0.6 s (windup 0.542-0.583 s, stretch 0.375 s). Each clip ends in the fielding position (glove up, knees bent). - Mechanics: leg lift (peak at frame 6 windup / 3 stretch), hip-shoulder separation (hips open to +20..34 deg while the shoulders are still closed at -20 deg at foot plant), arm path from the hand break (hand near the hip) through a cocked "ball up" position at foot plant to release, follow-through across the body, trailing leg swings through, ends fielding. Arm slots differ in arm path, trunk tilt (`side`) and lean: overhand (arm high, trunk tilt ~6 deg at release), three-quarter (~20), sidearm (arm horizontal, ~42, lower hips), submarine (arm below the waist, trunk bent ~64 forward + 50 sideways, hips ~0.4 m lower). Stretch versions use a shorter leg lift and stride (slide-step-like).
- **Foot-plant / mound convention:** the clip root is the ground plane at the **rubber top** (mound top, 0.254 m). Planted feet have their ankle at 0.085 m (sole on the ground). The mound falls 1 in per foot beginning 6 in in front of the rubber's front edge, so a foot planted `dy` metres ahead of the pivot foot stands at ankle z = 0.085 - 0.0833 * (dy - 0.23); the stride foot and the landing foot of every delivery follow that. Measured over every frame of every pitching clip the soles never sink below the mound surface by more than 14 mm (old `pitch` was 38 mm, now 5 mm). Place the pitcher at the mound-top height (0.254 m, the sim's `groundHeight` at the rubber) and do not add any further vertical offset; hip translation toward the plate is baked into the clips (root motion, up to 0.66 m).
- Hand/ball fit: the fist-like default hand pushed the ball 3 cm into the palm (that was the "awkward wrap"). `Hand_R_Ball` is an open claw grip: the ball centre sits at (x 0.058, y 0, z 0.110) in the hand frame (x palm normal, y thumb side, z along the hand), 47 finger/thumb contact vertices, no penetration (numerically checked). `Ball_Grip` orientation = 4-seam (the ball's glTF +Y, its seam symmetry axis, points to the thumb side, so the index/middle fingers lie across the horseshoe); `Ball_Grip_2Seam` = the ball turned 90 deg (axis along the fingers).

### Ball (`ball.glb`, `src/ball.py`, `src/ball_tex.py`)
The old ball wrapped an equirect UV sphere (texture pinch at the poles, coarse noise looking like a lumpy golf ball) and, in the hand, sat 3 cm inside the hand mesh. Verified by rendering eight orientations: the figure-8 shows correctly from every side, no pinching.

### Mound (`field.glb` `Mound`, `src/field.py` `mound_h`, `field_layout.json` `mound_profile`)
Profile, exact (metres, sim coordinates: x toward third, z toward center field, origin home plate apex):
`h(x,z) = max(0, min(cone, skirt))`, `cone = H - (1 in / 1 ft) * d`, `skirt = H * smoothstep((R - r) / 1.5 ft)`, `H = 10 in = 0.254 m`, `R = 9 ft` (18 ft diameter circle), `r = hypot(x, z - 59 ft)` (circle centre 59 ft from the apex), `d` = distance from the level rectangle `|x| <= 2.5 ft`, `60 ft <= z <= 60 ft + 34 in` (the rubber's front edge is at 60 ft 6 in). The front falls exactly 1 in per foot until the circle edge (10 ft in front of the level area, so h = 0 there); laterally and behind the surface follows the same cone but is cut by the skirt so the mound ends on its 18 ft circle. Sample values (m): (0, 17.98 = 59 ft) 0.229; (0, 16.5) 0.105; (0, 19.5) 0.225; (0, 20.5) 0.125; (2.5, 19) 0.007; (0, 18.29 .. 19.15) 0.254 (level top). The mesh is that function sampled on 72 rings x 128 segments (max deviation from the formula between vertices 2.8 cm on the steep rim), in absolute y (sim ground = 0) and clamped to y >= 0.028 so the rim sits just above the turf layers (dirt 0.010, grass 0.020/0.026, chalk 0.034): the glb value at the rim is 0.028, not 0.

**Comparison with `groundHeight` in `src/sim/field.ts`:** the level top (|x| <= 2.5 ft, MLB 34 in deep: `60 ft + 34 in = 19.152 m`, the sim uses `MOUND_DIST + 0.8 = 19.24 m`, 9 cm longer) and the front slope (1 in/ft for the first 6 ft) agree. The sim differs on the lateral/rear slopes because its profile is not limited by the 18 ft circle: it keeps falling 1 in/ft for 6 ft and then eases over a further 1.25 m measured from the level rectangle, so the sim's mound extends to about 12.5 ft laterally and 73 ft behind the apex (beyond the 18 ft circle that ends at 68 ft, x = 9 ft). The MLB rule only fixes the level area, the 6 in / 6 ft front slope and the 18 ft diameter, so the glb (mound inside its circle, level top and front slope exact) is the spec-compliant one; the sim should use the formula above (or ask for a different rear/lateral profile: it is one line in `field.py`).

- **Deployment size**: the deploy ships `optimized/` without `lod1/` (see `vite.config.ts`): about **100 MB** (players 90 MB, field 7.3, stadium 3.1, ball / bat < 1). The `lod1/` folder (players 49 MB + stadium 2.5 MB) is not deployed; the whole folder on disk is about 148 MB.
- **Face fixes (part 2c)**: the eye globes (`Eyes`, `Eyes_Cornea`) moved 8 mm back into the sockets (they sat 1-7 mm in front of the lid surface: startled stare, and `eyes_blink` could not cover them; both eyes now close completely at `eyes_blink` 1; `eyes_look_*` pivots follow). `mouth_open` is rescaled (weight 1 = 30 % of the raw MPFB opening, a natural talking / shouting mouth): beyond that the MPFB mouth interior (stretched inner-lip faces) shows as jagged spikes, which is why the earlier "teeth look jagged" caveat. `Gear_Teeth` / `Gear_Tongue` now move rigidly with the upper lip / the lower jaw (they were torn apart by the nearest-surface field). Beards (`Gear_Beard_Full`, `Gear_Mustache`, `Gear_Goatee`) use their own opaque fibre material `hair_beard` (they used the alpha hair-card material and came out as torn shards; recolour with the other `hair*` materials); `Gear_Beard_Stubble` is unchanged (flat translucent brown, a hard upper edge, no soft fade). Known remaining face issues: the default short hair has a ragged hairline on the forehead and a rough nape edge (hidden by caps / helmets), there are no mouth-interior / gum meshes (open mouths show the stock card teeth and a hollow head behind), no hero (4K, subdivided) head variant, no extra age / tone sets beyond the manager's.
