Tips for a great recording
  • Pop filter (or a sock over the mic) and keep the mic ~10-15 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
  • Same spot every session: same mic, position, distance, room, chair. Do not change the gain between sessions.
  • Quiet room: soft furnishings help; turn off fans, fridges, and phones. Capture room tone each time.
  • Peaks around -12 to -3 dB on the meter. Never red. Shout the big calls from a little further back.
  • Let the last word finish and die away before stopping; the app adds the quiet padding for you.
  • Do the style in the direction line. Stretch the vowels, let the energy rise. Perform it; do not read it.
  • Hydrate, take a break every 15-20 minutes, stop when your voice gets tired: the first session sounds different from the fifth.
  • Mistake? Just retake (←). Do not splice or repair, retakes are free.
