Also fixed: the broadcast's B-roll plan differed between 30 and 60 fps (its seed advanced on every planning retry; 0981ae4).

Capture-only problems (the promo runtime, not the game): the loading screen's fade-out was frozen into the first frames when the virtual
clock took over CSS animations (now finished before the switch); a real mouse moving over the headed capture window opened the player's
control bar over the broadcast (the controls are hidden while capturing); a headed window that the desktop hid stalled page screenshots for a
minute (captures run headless on the GPU now); the portrait "Rotate your device" hint is dismissed in captures, as a player would.

Edit problems (the promo tools): a booth track laid over several cuts had a fixed length, and each take times its words differently, so
the 1080p bomb call ended 0.28 s into the analyst's "Wow!" (and the vertical take's call was cut mid-word too). Found in the voice stem's
spectrogram; a booth track now ends at a pause: it finishes a word that ends within 0.6 s, else stops at the last pause (`speechEnd` in
`dsp.ts`, `speechEnd.test.ts`), and the captions use the same window (2d86ae1).

## Known, not fixed here (for the coordinator)
- **The swing plane is too steep**: 60 ms before contact the bat points almost straight down (seed 40: -88 deg, the barrel tip 0.24 m off the
  ground; seed 30: -80 deg) and it meets the ball at -43 to -54 deg (a real swing is roughly -25 to -35 deg). It reads as a chop in slow motion.
  Measured with the sim's `bat.knob` / `bat.tip`; changing the swing model changes every outcome, so it belongs in a sim thread.
- Caps sit high on the head with the bill tilted up, so from the front a cap reads as a beanie and the whole forehead shows (a model / fit
  question for the asset pipeline; the promo keeps faces to one shot from slightly off-axis).
- No diving catch exists: the sim has no dive for fielders (only `dive_back` for runners), so the promo shows lunges and wall leaps instead.
- Steals: 19 successful steals in 240 scouted games and none avoided a tag (`tagAvoided` never fired on a steal).
- Bang-bang plays at first (`closePlay`, |margin| < 0.10 s) are rare: 1 in 240 games.
